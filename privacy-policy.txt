Privacy Policy for See Translation Keys

Data Collection:
This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or user information.

Local Storage:
The extension stores only one piece of data locally: a boolean value indicating whether showing translation keys is enabled or disabled. This data remains on your device and is never shared or transmitted.

Permissions:
- storage: Used to remember your on/off preference across browser sessions

How it works:
When enabled, the extension rewrites GetUilmFile translation responses in the page so each value is replaced with its key. No data is sent off the device.

No Third-Party Services:
This extension does not communicate with any external servers or third-party services.
